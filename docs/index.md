Startup Analyst Agent is an AI-powered business intelligence assistant that evaluates startups by analyzing market potential, business models, competition, traction, and risks. It provides structured insights, SWOT analysis, growth opportunities, and data-driven recommendations—helping founders, investors, and analysts make informed strategic decisions.
